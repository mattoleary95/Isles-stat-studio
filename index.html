Stats source

Stats update automatically from NHL.com every morning. To use your own sheet instead (for columns NHL.com doesn't have, like xGF%), open the Forwards tab, click the corner box above row 1 to select everything, copy, and paste here. A pasted tab replaces the NHL.com stats for that position until you switch back.

Setup file

Stat picks, photos, team logos and jersey numbers are remembered in this browser. Save a setup file to keep a backup or move it to another computer. Import team logos reads any setup file that has a logos list (keyed by team abbreviation, like NYR).

Logos & headshots

Team logos

Click a team to upload its logo (a PNG with a transparent background looks best). Logos sit behind that team's players on comparison cards. The Islanders tile sets the logo behind Isles players; without one, Up The Turnpike is used.

Headshots

Cut-out PNG headshots (transparent background) look best: the team logo shows behind the player.

Stats come from NHL.com's public stats API and MoneyPuck.com (advanced stats: xG, xGF%, Corsi, GSAx, high-danger), refreshed every morning. Graphics that show a MoneyPuck stat carry a "Data: MoneyPuck.com" credit, as MoneyPuck asks. League AVG is the average NHL regular at the position (players with at least half the league-high games played). League AVG tags compare against each tab's League AVG row.
